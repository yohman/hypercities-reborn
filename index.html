    Fly / Walk

    W A S D move. Arrow keys turn and look. Q / E change altitude in Fly. Hold Shift to move faster.

    In Walk, drag to look or use “Look around” for pointer capture. Esc releases the pointer. Buildings are contemporary OSM footprints with recorded or estimated heights. Contemporary footprints around the Palast study are hidden while its volume is active. Movement follows a simplified ellipsoid surface; building collision and historical terrain are not modeled.

    Timewell

    Drag the temporal section to scrub. Scroll or use the scale control to move continuously from millennia to seconds. Focus the section and use ← / → to scrub; + / − to zoom. Click the date to enter an exact date, including “500 BCE”.

    Click an encounter to inspect it. “Enter this moment” explicitly travels to its saved viewpoint. Changing time alone preserves your position. Map following can be switched off.

    Evidence / studies

    This is a local interface study, with no database. Historical dates and sources are separate from media playback. The Palast model is an approximate massing study; the video is an animated map study; the sound is a synthetic sound study. None is archival testimony.

    Time uses a proleptic Gregorian calendar and UTC. BCE display omits year zero. Dates without known times remain labeled by year. The time control’s precision does not imply precision in the evidence.
